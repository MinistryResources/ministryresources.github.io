Night Church is an opportunity for people to gather, hear God's word, encourage one another and respond to the good news of Jesus. As a Service Leader, you'll help make that happen.

The big picture

A deep, warm service.

The way we lead matters. We want people to be able to engage with God's word, sing and pray together, and be encouraged to keep following Jesus. A thoughtful welcome, a clear introduction or a well-led moment in the service can help people take part and feel at home.

Deep We want service leaders to point people to the good news of Jesus. We don't want people distracted by forced jokes, trivial comments, or anything else that takes away from our gospel focus.
Warm At the same time, we don't want to be cold and impersonal! We want a genuine, welcoming and warm presence up front. It's great to be relaxed and have a laugh along the way.
What you'll do

Leading together

You'll be paired with a co-leader (most likely a pastor). Together, you'll prepare and lead the service, making sure each part fits together and helps the congregation engage.

01

Get ready

Check out the Bible passage / topic for that week to help you get a sense of the overall direction of the service.

02

Plan with your co-leader

Talk during the week about how you'll lead each part of the service together. Your Elvanto dashboard will have a link to the service plan.

03

Lead on the night

Arrive ready for the team briefing (by 5.50pm) and lead the service alongside your co-leader.

04

Reflect and keep growing

Take part in any opportunities to debrief offered by your leaders, so that you can reflect and grow as a service leader.

You don't need to know it all already.

This role is a chance to give service leading a go, with someone alongside you. We don't expect you to service lead perfectly! Instead, we want to help you make progress as you lead.
